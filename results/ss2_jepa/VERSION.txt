  crossovers held piecewise-constant:

    tile     abs {0.01, 0.01, 0.02}  scat 0.10
    carpet   abs {0.30, 0.50, 0.60}  scat 0.50
    mirror   abs {0.00, 0.00, 0.00}  scat 0.00

  Materials bind through the semantic-mesh chain; RLR matches a material by its
  "name" field against the semantic CLASS name, so each material is named after
  the surface label it is applied to.
